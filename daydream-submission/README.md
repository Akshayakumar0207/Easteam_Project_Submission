# Daydream Skirt redesign: EASTEAM intern task (Akshaya Kumar)

## Files
- `index.html`: the redesigned product page (click S / M / L to see stock, delivery date and button change)
- `audit.html`: top 5 improvements, the number one fix, process, tools, time
- `Akshaya_Kumar_EASTEAM_Application.docx`: all 20 questionnaire answers plus the practical task

## Before you send
1. Open `audit.html` in a text editor and replace the "Time taken" line with your real time.
2. Open the .docx and fill every [bracket]: city, start date, hours per week, what you built in One Tap and StudySphere, your Q17 story, time taken.
3. Open `index.html` by double-clicking it, and check it on your phone too.

## Put it online (free, about 2 minutes)
1. Go to vercel.com/new and sign in.
2. Drag this whole folder onto the page (or run `npx vercel` inside it).
3. Vercel gives you a link such as `your-project.vercel.app`. Open it in a private window to confirm it loads, then paste it in the form.
